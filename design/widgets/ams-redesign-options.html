AMS widget: three redesigns

Same demo printers in each. Every option keeps the per-widget printer choice and the "updated … ago" stamp, and draws effects (sparkle, multicolor, translucent) as the app does.

ASpool Rack

Each slot is drawn as a spool seen side-on: a ring of the filament's colour around a hub, material underneath. The slot printing right now glows and says "Feeding", so you can see which colour is in use. An empty slot is a dashed outline.

Needs: which slot is feeding (the app has it; added to the widget's data).

BAMS Front

Each AMS unit looks like the real box: a dark unit with four tall windows of filament, material written up the side. The unit header shows its humidity (orange when damp) and a "Drying" tag while it dries. The feeding slot has a small light, like the AMS's own.

Needs: AMS humidity, drying state and the feeding slot (the app has all three; added to the widget's data).

CStock Check

Built around "will this spool last?": each slot shows its colour name, what's left in grams, and a bar that turns orange below the low-stock level. Small shows the four slots as rings that empty as the spool does. Large adds a count of low spools at the top.

Needs: each loaded spool's colour name, remaining amount and low-stock flag from your inventory (the app has them; added to the widget's data). A slot with no inventory spool shows its material only.
